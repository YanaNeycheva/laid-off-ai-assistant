Mods for comeback users

Five static mockups for the people who use /comeback after a layoff. They show the person's own situation, read from their Personal/<date>-<slug>/dossier.md, in Bulgarian. The persona ("Мария") and every date and amount are invented sample data, not verified legal facts.

The deadlines that cost money if missed, as days left. The band turns amber, then red, as a date gets close. Only facts the freshness-checker confirmed are shown, each with its "проверено на" date.
